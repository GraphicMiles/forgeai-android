The Mr Nobody pipeline, ported

Nothing is a scripted animation. The buttons append events to the log below, the log is projected by a port of TaskTimeline.fromEvents, and the projection is drawn by a port of agent_response.dart. A row exists only because an event created it.

Copied exactly

  • PixelLoader — 3×3, 4px cells, 1.5px gaps, 650ms, per-cell delay (col + |row−1|) × 90ms.
  • ShimmerLabel — a 1.4s sweep that stops dead rather than slowing.
  • AgentTrace — expanded while running, folded to “Thought for N seconds” once settled, chevron always drawn, the rail a hairline on the left.
  • Seven states — working, done, recovered, failed, denied, waiting, cancelled, each with its own marker.
  • fromEvents — current-run slicing, one row per step.changed, tool calls as evidence beneath the row, call-id pairing with a fallback to the latest open attempt, and _metric with its singulariser.
  • StreamedAnswer — word by word out of 4px of blur, hard on/off caret.

Changed for Luna

  • Standard chat layout: user right in the filled bubble, Luna left with the mark in the gutter and one bubble holding the trace and the answer. Actions and follow-ups sit outside the bubble, on its left edge.
  • Paddings pulled in — bubble 9×11, trace rows 4px, detail 6×8, approval 9×10.
  • The approval card fits its content and Skip is legible.
  • The unsupported-figure note is ink on a plain surface. No amber anywhere.
  • Streaming slowed to 160ms a word.
