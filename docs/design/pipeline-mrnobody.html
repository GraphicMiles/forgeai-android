The Mr Nobody pipeline, ported value for value

Nothing here is scripted UI. The buttons append events to a log, the log is projected by a line-for-line port of TaskTimeline.fromEvents, and the projection is rendered by a port of agent_response.dart. A row exists only because an event created it.

What was copied, exactly

  • AgentMetrics — 13px body × 1.62 = a 21px line box, a 21px avatar column with a 19px mark centred in it, 10px column gap, 31px indent, 20px between turns, 16px gutter, cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1) on every entrance.
  • PixelLoader — 3×3 grid, 4px cells, 1.5px gaps, 650ms period, per-cell delay (col + |row−1|) × 90ms, so two wavefronts are always in flight.
  • ShimmerLabel — 1.4s sweep that stops dead rather than slowing, and goes faint when the work it describes is over.
  • AgentWorkingLine — loader, shimmering label, elapsed counter ticking every 100ms in tabular figures.
  • AgentTrace — auto-expanded while running, folds to “Thought for N seconds” when settled, chevron always drawn (hover never fires on a touch screen), the rail is a 1px left border, rows are 16px marker + 7px + label, status line reads working · metric · duration.
  • The seven states — working, done, recovered, failed, denied, waiting, cancelled, each with its own marker, straight out of TimelineState.
  • TaskTimeline.fromEvents — current-run slicing at the last task.started, one row per step.changed, tool calls as evidence nested under the row, call-id pairing with a fallback to the latest open attempt, _settledState (waiting beats denied beats recovered beats failed), and _metric with its singulariser.
  • StreamedAnswer — word by word out of 4px of blur over 420ms, hard on/off caret.
  • Sources — collected only from tool calls that really read a page, drawn as lettermarks. Never a favicon request.
  • FigureWarning — the amber note when a number in the answer is not on the pages read.

Where it lands on Luna, and the two conflicts

  • Free fixes. “1 lines” disappears — _metric singularises the unit. The raw JSON leak disappears — a tool call is an event, and events render as rows, never as text in the bubble. “Working · 1 step” while parked disappears — waiting is a state on the row, and the status line stops shimmering. The missing write step appears, because the row is created by the call, not by the result.
  • Conflict 1 — amber. FigureWarning is a second colour, and Luna's rule is one colour outside the mascot. Say the word and it becomes ink on a plain surface.
  • Conflict 2 — approval. Mr Nobody has no approval widget in this file; the pause lives in ApprovalPolicy. The card here is mine, sized to its content with Skip at full contrast, and it appears the moment the row turns waiting.
