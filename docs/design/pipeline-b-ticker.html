B — The ticker

One line, always in the same place, saying the one thing happening now. The list of everything that happened is a receipt you can open, not the main event.

The animation idea

  • Text rolls, it does not swap. The old activity blurs up and out, the new one rises in from below — 420ms, so you register the change without reading it twice.
  • Progress is a row of hairlines. One bead per step, filling left to right. The live bead has the highlight running through it; a finished one is solid. The strip is the only thing that grows.
  • Approval comes from the composer, not the middle of the thread — it rises 14px over the input, because that is where your thumb already is.
  • Nothing collapses at the end: the ticker just stops and becomes 4 steps · 11.5s, tappable for the receipt.

What it fixes from your screenshot

  • The thread stays a conversation. No {"tool":…}, no tall card pushing the answer off screen.
  • The approval never covers the trace, and both buttons read at full contrast.
  • Waiting on you has its own colour of language — the beads freeze.
