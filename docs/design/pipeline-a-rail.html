A — The rail

The trace is the spine of the answer. Steps hang off a single hairline that grows downward as work happens, and the whole block folds into one line the moment the job is done.

The animation idea

  • Rows arrive, they do not appear. Each step slides up 6px over 340ms on the same easing the sheets use.
  • One thing shimmers at a time. The active label has a highlight travelling through it; the instant it finishes the shimmer stops and the text goes solid. Nothing on screen moves without a reason.
  • The tick is the reward. The dot fills from grey to ink with a 300ms colour lerp — the only "yes" in the interface.
  • Folding is the summary. On completion the rows collapse with grid-template-rows: 1fr → 0fr and the header rewrites itself to Thought for 11.5s. Tap to open it again.
  • The answer arrives out of a blur, a word every 100ms.

What it fixes from your screenshot

  • The raw {"tool":…} never reaches the thread — a tool call is a row, not text.
  • Approval is a card a third of the height, with Skip in a solid grey that is legible instead of ghosted.
  • "1 lines" is now "1 line".
  • The status line says Waiting on you and the pixels stop moving.
