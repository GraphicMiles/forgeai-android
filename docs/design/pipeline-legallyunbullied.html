The Legallyunbullied pipeline, ported

Five uneven stages on a timer, not a progress bar. The trace opens itself at stage 1, shows the first two rows at stage 2, the rest at stage 3, and folds at stage 4 into Thought for X.Xs. Same numbers, same easing, same four variants.

STAGES = [800, 600, 1800, 2600, 1600] ms
idle—
0 header only, shimmering · 1 trace opens, empty · 2 first two rows · 3 all rows, last one settles · 4 folded, elapsed frozen.
Uneven on purpose: an even cadence reads as a progress bar, and a progress bar is a promise.

Copied exactly

  • The stage machine — [800, 600, 1800, 2600, 1600], autoExpanded = stage ≥ 1 && stage < 4, working = stage < 3, rows revealed 0 → 2 → all.
  • Shimmer — shimmer-text 1.4s linear infinite over a 200% gradient, swapped for a 350ms fade when the label settles.
  • Expansion — grid-template-rows 1fr/0fr at 400ms cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1), chevron rotating 180° over 300ms.
  • The measured rail — a 1px line positioned at left:3px, its height measured from the rows after paint and animated over 500ms, so it grows with the trace instead of jumping.
  • Rows — fade-up 320ms staggered i × 120ms, 12.5px primary, 11.5px secondary, spinner on the working row and a check behind it.
  • Live elapsed — ticked every 500ms from startedAt, then frozen as a fixed elapsedMs once done, which is what makes a reloaded message render its finished state instantly.
  • Streaming — each word starts at opacity 0, blur(10px), translateY(4px) and resolves over stream-in 500ms cubic-bezier(.22,.61,.25,1). Actions fade in over 400ms after the last word; follow-ups fade up staggered 90ms.
  • The citation rule — a chip every 15 words, and only when a real source exists. Their own note: never fabricate a placeholder domain.

Changed for Luna

  • No colour. Their Search dots are accent/amber/green and their tool counts are green/red. Here they are one ink at different weights.
  • No loop. HOLD_MS = 3400 and the replay are a marketing demo. One shot only.
  • Slower stream. 170ms a word instead of 100.
  • An approval stage. Their machine has no pause; “With approval” holds at stage 2 until you answer, and the elapsed clock stops while it waits.
  • Tighter chrome — 26px rows, 9×11 bubble, cards that fit their content.
