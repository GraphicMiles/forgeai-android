C — The focus card

One card. It never grows — it changes into the next thing. Finished steps slide underneath it as thin bars, so you can see the job has depth without reading a list.

The animation idea

  • The card morphs. Icon, label and detail cross-fade out of a 5px blur while the card keeps its position — the eye never has to re-find it.
  • Done work sinks. Each finished step drops 6px behind the card and loses opacity, building a stack you can feel rather than count.
  • The tick pops. The icon circle fills and overshoots to 1.18 for 160ms. That is the only bounce in the app.
  • Waiting is a ring, not a spinner. When Luna needs you the filled circle becomes a hollow ring that breathes at 2.1s — slow, patient, and visibly different from the 650ms working rhythm.
  • The end state is one pill: 4 steps · 11.5s. Tap it and the full list unfolds.

What it fixes from your screenshot

  • Nothing accumulates. The tallest thing on screen is 60px, so the answer is always visible.
  • A tool call is never printed as JSON — it is the label on the card.
  • "Working · 1 step" while nothing is happening is impossible: the card says what it is doing or it says it is waiting for you.
